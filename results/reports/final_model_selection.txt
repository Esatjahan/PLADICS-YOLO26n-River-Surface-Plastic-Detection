3. Experimental Refinement Summary
---------------------------------

Several controlled experiments were conducted to improve performance:

- Label normalization and cleaning (critical improvement step)
- Removal of corrupt annotations
- Stable validation pipeline established
- High-resolution training (imgsz=768) → performance degraded
- Aggressive augmentation → unstable results
- Controlled fine-tuning → no significant gain

Key finding:
→ Data quality improvement had the largest impact on performance

5. Interpretation of Results
----------------------------

YOLO26n_v2 achieved the highest performance across all major metrics.

This indicates:

- improved detection reliability
- reduced false positives
- significantly improved localization accuracy
- better generalization after dataset cleaning

Compared to initial YOLO26n baseline, improvements include:

- higher precision
- higher recall
- strong increase in mAP@0.5:0.95 (localization quality)

------------------------------------------------------------

6. Deployment-Oriented Consideration
-----------------------------------

The PLADICS system focuses on real-time detection of floating plastic.

Key priorities:

- high precision (avoid false actions)
- stable detection
- accurate localization

Single-class detection is sufficient for deployment since:

- plastic presence detection is the primary goal
- classification of plastic type is not required for rover action

------------------------------------------------------------

7. Final Model Selection
-----------------------

Selected Model (Deployment):
YOLO26n_v2

Alternative Model:
YOLO11n

------------------------------------------------------------

8. Justification
----------------

YOLO26n_v2 is selected as the final deployment model based on:

- highest precision among all models
- highest mAP@0.5 and mAP@0.5:0.95
- improved localization performance
- stable and reproducible validation results
- robustness after dataset cleaning

These characteristics make it highly suitable for real-time robotic deployment.

YOLO11n remains a strong alternative due to slightly higher recall in earlier experiments.

------------------------------------------------------------

9. Multiclass Experiment Context
-------------------------------

Additional multiclass experiments (RiSID and Riverine) showed:

- expected performance drop due to increased task complexity
- dataset-dependent variation
- stronger results on larger datasets (RiSID)

These experiments support research analysis but are not used for deployment selection.
