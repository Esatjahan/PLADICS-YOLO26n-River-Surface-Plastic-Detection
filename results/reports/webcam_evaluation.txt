PLADICS Webcam Evaluation Report
================================

Model Evaluated:
YOLO26n (Single-Class, Merged Dataset)

Evaluation Setup:
- Input: Laptop webcam (real-time video)
- Confidence threshold: 0.40
- Environment: indoor / normal lighting conditions

------------------------------------------------------------

Detection Performance:

- The model successfully detects plastic objects in real-time.
- Detection is stable across consecutive frames.
- Bounding boxes remain consistent and do not flicker significantly.

------------------------------------------------------------

Confidence Behavior:

- Confidence values are generally consistent.
- Most detections appear above the threshold (0.40).
- High-confidence detections (>0.70) are stable and reliable.

------------------------------------------------------------

False Positives:

- The model produces relatively low false positives.
- Occasional incorrect detections may occur on visually similar objects.
- Overall false detection rate is acceptable for deployment.

------------------------------------------------------------

Real-Time Performance:

- The model runs smoothly in real-time.
- Frame processing is fast enough for continuous monitoring.
- No major lag observed during inference.

------------------------------------------------------------

Robustness:

- Detection performance remains stable under slight camera movement.
- Performance is affected under poor lighting conditions, but still functional.

------------------------------------------------------------

Conclusion:

YOLO26n demonstrates stable and reliable real-time detection performance.

It provides:
- consistent detection
- acceptable confidence levels
- low false positives
- smooth real-time operation

Therefore, it is suitable for deployment in the PLADICS system for real-time plastic detection.
