PLADICS Deployment Decision
==========================

Final Selected Model:
YOLO26n (Single-Class, Merged Dataset)

Reason:
- Highest overall weighted score among all models
- Highest precision, resulting in fewer false positives
- Strong mAP@0.5:0.95 indicating better localization accuracy
- Stable real-time performance observed during webcam testing

Deployment Objective:
The primary objective of PLADICS is to detect the presence of floating plastic for collection, not to classify plastic subtypes.

Therefore, single-class detection is more aligned with real-world deployment.

Multiclass Role:
Multiclass models (RiSID and Riverine) were trained for research validation and category-level analysis, but are not used for deployment.

Conclusion:
YOLO26n trained on the merged single-class dataset is selected as the final deployment model.
