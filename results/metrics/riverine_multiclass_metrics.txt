Riverine Multiclass (YOLO26n)
============================

Dataset:
Riverine Plastic Litter Dataset (Multiclass)

Overall Metrics:
- Precision: ~0.564
- Recall: ~0.659
- mAP@0.5: ~0.714
- mAP@0.5:0.95: ~0.396

Observations:
- Dataset is small (~142 annotated samples)
- Class imbalance present
- Lower performance compared to RiSID
- plastic_cup class shows weaker detection

Conclusion:
Riverine dataset is useful for validation and generalization testing, but not strong enough for primary training.
